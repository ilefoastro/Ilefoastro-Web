How it works

1. Tell us

Message us about your business and send your name, services, photos, hours and WhatsApp number.

2. Start

Pay 50% to start. You get a receipt. We build it in about 3 to 5 days (5 to 7 for portfolio sites). You can reserve your slot with a small booking deposit, which counts towards the 50%. We confirm the rest on WhatsApp.

Pay deposit

3. Approve and publish

You check the link and approve in writing. You pay the other 50% and we publish.

We publish your name, photos and phone number only after you approve them. We help you become easier to find, but we do not promise Google rankings or a number of customers. We never ask for your passwords. Your accounts stay yours.

Before you pay

Is hosting included?

Yes. Your site is hosted for free on fast, secure hosting. There is no yearly hosting fee from us.

Is a domain name included?

No. The free web address works from day one. A custom domain such as .com.ng is optional. You pay the registrar directly, including the yearly renewal. We can help set it up, and we confirm any fee first.

What about changes after launch?

One free round of small text, price or photo changes within 14 days of going live. After that, small edits are quoted per request, and we confirm the price before we start.

Who owns the site?

You do. The accounts are in your name and the files are yours. If you want to move, we hand everything over.
